proc
hasChanged
(update_now, update_delay_buffer)

Updates the chunk, makes sure that it doesn't update too much. If the chunk isn't being watched it will instead be flagged to update the next time an AI Eye moves near it.

update_delay_buffer is used for cameras that are moving around, which are cyborg inbuilt cameras and mecha onboard cameras. This buffer should be usually lower than the cameranet's chunk_update_delay because otherwise a moving camera can run out of its own view before updating static.
